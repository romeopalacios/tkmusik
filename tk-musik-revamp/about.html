About TK Musik

We go on stage as a team, go home as a family.

TK Musik Akademy is built around musicianship, community, mentorship and real performance experience.

Our Variety Is Our Strength

We teach, nurture and mentor students while giving them opportunities to become musicians on stages ranging from casual to formal settings.

We Work As A Community

As a small business, we focus on building a tight community where everyone connects on both a personal and professional level, supported by talented teachers.

Meet the musical director

Ty Khuong

Ty Khuong, Music Director, Owner and Lead Instructor, has been in the music business for over 20 years, with teaching experience spanning young beginners from age 4 through students in their early 80s.

Passion is what drives a thriving career, devotion is what keeps a successful business, and discipline is key to maintaining exceptional relationships with everyone.
